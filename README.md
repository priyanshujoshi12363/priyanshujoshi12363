

# 💫 About Me:
Hello, I'm Priyanshu Joshi, a second-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning student at Parul University, Vadodara. I am a passionate developer, builder, and aspiring entrepreneur who enjoys transforming ideas into real-world products through software, AI, and embedded systems.<br><br>My primary expertise lies in Full Stack Development, AI/ML, IoT, and Backend Engineering. I have experience working with technologies such as Node.js, Express.js, React, React Native, MongoDB, TypeScript, Python, and Firebase. Alongside software development, I actively work with hardware platforms including ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors, GPS modules, and embedded AI systems.<br><br>Over the years, I have built and contributed to multiple projects ranging from mobile applications and AI platforms to logistics solutions and smart IoT systems. One of my major projects is Sukobin, a logistics and parcel delivery platform designed specifically for hilly regions such as Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, and Northeast India. The platform focuses on solving transportation challenges in difficult terrains through intelligent route planning and driver matching.<br><br>I am also developing CodeSeek V2, an AI-powered platform aimed at improving software development workflows, and an Embedded AI Platform focused on simplifying IoT and embedded application development. In addition, I have worked on projects such as ChatterCub, Karigar, RapidDonor, BookIt, and several AI-powered applications involving recommendation systems, conversational AI, and automation.<br><br>Beyond development, I actively participate in hackathons and innovation challenges. I was selected as a finalist in the India AI Impact Buildathon, competing among thousands of participants across the country. These experiences have strengthened my problem-solving abilities, teamwork skills, and product-thinking mindset.<br><br>My interests extend into machine learning, deep learning, operating systems, computer architecture, embedded systems, ARM processors, and scalable backend systems. I enjoy learning how technology works from the lowest hardware level to large-scale cloud deployments.<br><br>My long-term goal is not just to become a software engineer but to build impactful technology products and startups that solve meaningful problems. I aim to establish successful ventures around my projects, contribute to innovation in India, and create solutions that can improve people's daily lives.<br><br>I strongly believe in continuous learning, experimentation, and execution. Whether it's building an AI application, designing an IoT device, creating a startup MVP, or participating in a hackathon, I enjoy taking ideas from concept to reality.<br>
