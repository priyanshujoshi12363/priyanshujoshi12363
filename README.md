# 💫 About Me

Hey there! I'm **Priyanshu Joshi**, a second-year B.Tech student in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** at **Parul University, Vadodara**. I'm a passionate developer, builder, and aspiring entrepreneur who loves turning ideas into real-world products through software, AI, and embedded systems.

### 🚀 What I Do

My primary expertise spans **Full Stack Development**, **AI/ML**, **IoT**, and **Backend Engineering**. I work extensively with modern technologies including Node.js, Express.js, React, React Native, MongoDB, TypeScript, Python, and Firebase. On the hardware side, I tinker with ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors, GPS modules, and embedded AI systems.

### 🛠️ Featured Projects

- **Sukobin** — A logistics and parcel delivery platform designed for challenging terrains like Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, and Northeast India. Features intelligent route planning and driver matching to solve last-mile delivery in hilly regions.

- **CodeSeek V2** — An AI-powered platform that streamlines software development workflows with intelligent code assistance and automation.

- **Embedded AI Platform** — Simplifying IoT and embedded application development by bridging the gap between hardware and AI.

- **Other Projects** — ChatterCub, Karigar, RapidDonor, BookIt, and various AI applications involving recommendation systems, conversational AI, and automation.

### 🏆 Achievements

- **Finalist** at the **India AI Impact Buildathon**, competing among thousands of participants nationwide.
- Active hackathon participant with a strong track record of building MVPs under pressure.

### 🌱 Learning & Interests

I'm deeply curious about the full technology stack — from bare-metal embedded systems and ARM processors to scalable cloud deployments. My interests include:
- Machine Learning & Deep Learning
- Operating Systems & Computer Architecture
- Embedded Systems & IoT
- Scalable Backend Architecture

### 🎯 Vision

My goal isn't just to be a software engineer — I want to build impactful technology products and startups that solve real problems. I'm working towards establishing successful ventures around my projects, contributing to India's innovation ecosystem, and creating solutions that improve people's daily lives.

I live by the mantra: **Learn continuously, experiment fearlessly, and execute relentlessly.**
